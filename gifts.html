💖 A Naomi Promise

To my absolute favorite human, Kiye, I wanted to take a moment to write this down because sometimes life gets so busy, and I want to make sure you always know exactly how much you mean to me. You aren't just my best friend; you are my chosen family, my safe harbor, and the person who makes this world a whole lot brighter just by being in it. Looking back at everything we’ve been through—all the late-night laughs that made our stomachs hurt, the quiet moments when we didn't need words at all, and the times we lifted each other up when things got heavy—I am just so incredibly grateful for you. So, I want to make a few promises to you today, Kiye, and I intend to keep them for the rest of our lives. I promise to always listen to you, whether you need to vent about the same thing for the hundredth time, cry over a heartbreak, or share a random, chaotic midnight thought, because my phone and my heart are always open to you. I promise to be your biggest cheerleader because your dreams are my dreams, and I will always be the loudest person cheering for you in the front row of your life, celebrating your wins like they are my own. Anything you entrust to me is locked away forever, and I promise to hold your secrets safe so you never have to filter yourself or worry about judgment with me. On the days when the world gets too loud and you start to doubt your own worth, I promise to remind you of who you are, holding up a mirror to show you how incredibly strong, kind, and capable you are. Most of all, I promise to show up through the good times, the bad times, and the incredibly boring in-between times, because if you need me at 3:00 AM, I am there. No matter where life takes us, how much we grow, or how many miles end up between us, nothing will ever change the bond we share. You have a permanent piece of my heart, Kiye, and I am so lucky to walk through this life with you. Thank you for being the extraordinary person you are. I love you to the moon and back, always and forever. No matter how much time passes, I’ll always be grateful that I met you. That won’t change.
