Flowers For You

Each flower opens only when you need it. Pick one whenever your heart feels heavy or bright.

🌷 Open when you feel sad

You’re allowed to feel everything deeply — it’s part of what makes you so human and so beautiful. I’m here, even when I’m not right next to you.

🌼 Open when you need a reminder

You matter more than you think. You are loved more than you realise. You are stronger than you feel today.

🌸 Open when you can’t sleep

Breathe. Slow. The world isn’t going anywhere. You’re safe. You’re okay. I’m thinking of you.

🌹 Open when you feel alone

You’re never alone — not really. I’m always one message, one call, one thought away.

🌻 Open when you’re happy

I hope your joy feels warm and bright and endless. You deserve every good thing that finds you.

💐 Open when you miss me

I miss you too. And I’m grateful every day that our paths crossed the way they did.
