A Letter to Your Future Self

For the version of you who’s still growing, still glowing, still becoming.

Dear Future Kiye,

I don’t know exactly when you’ll read this, but I hope it finds you in a moment where you can breathe, smile, and feel how deeply loved you are. I’m writing this for the future — for the version of us who has lived a little more, learned a little more, and hopefully stayed just as close as we are right now. Sometimes I think about how friendships change as life moves forward. People grow, drift, reconnect, transform — but with you, I’ve always felt something different. Something steady. Something that feels like home. And I want you to know that no matter how much time passes, no matter what new chapters we step into, I want us to still be besties. Not just in the casual way people say it, but in the real way — the “I know your laugh, your moods, your dreams, your chaos, and your heart” kind of way. You’ve been such a huge part of my life already. You’ve seen me in moments where I didn’t even fully understand myself, and you stayed. You’ve made me laugh on days when I didn’t think I could. You’ve listened to me, supported me, teased me, hyped me up, and reminded me who I am when I forget. That’s not something time can erase. That’s something time strengthens. I hope future us still sends each other random messages about the most unimportant things. I hope we still have inside jokes that no one else understands. I hope we still talk about our dreams — the big ones, the small ones, the ones that scare us a bit. I hope we still show up for each other, even when life gets busy or messy or confusing. I hope we still choose each other as friends, again and again. And if the future ever gets heavy for you, I hope you remember this: you don’t have to carry anything alone. Not now, not later. I want to be someone who walks beside you through every version of your life — the bright parts, the uncertain parts, the parts you’re proud of, and the parts you’re still figuring out. You deserve someone who stays, and I want to be that person. I don’t know what the future looks like, but I know this: I want you in it. I want us laughing about how dramatic we used to be. I want us looking back at this letter and thinking, “Wow, we really made it through so much — and we’re still here.” I want us to still be best friends in the kind of way that feels unshakeable. So here’s to future us — older, wiser, maybe a little more chaotic, but still connected. Still choosing each other. Still besties. I hope you’re smiling as you read this — the kind of smile that reaches your eyes. I hope life feels softer than it used to, and that you’ve kept the parts of yourself that make you who you are: your warmth, your humour, your stubborn little spark.

I hope you’ve learned to rest without guilt, to love without fear, and to trust that you deserve good things simply because you exist.

With love, Your bestest friend💐🥹
