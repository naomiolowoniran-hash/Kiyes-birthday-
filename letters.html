Dear Kiye,

My beautiful Kiye, Happy birthday, my love. I hope you’re ready, because I’m about to write you the longest, most emotional, most dramatic letter you’ve ever received in your life. And honestly? You deserve every single word. I’ve been thinking about you all day — about how lucky I am to know you, how grateful I am that you exist, how much brighter my world is because you’re in it. And the more I think about it, the more I realise that there aren’t enough words in the entire world to describe how much you mean to me. But I’m going to try anyway, because you deserve to hear it all. You are one of the most important people in my life. You’re not just my best friend — you’re my safe place, my comfort, my joy, my laughter, my calm, my chaos, my everything. You’re the person I can talk to about anything, the person I can be stupid with, the person I can be emotional with, the person I can trust with the parts of me I don’t show anyone else. You’re the one who makes me feel understood without me having to explain myself. You’re the one who makes me feel seen. And I love you, Kiye. I love you so much. I love you in a way that feels big and warm and overflowing. I love you in a way that makes me want to protect you, hype you up, support you, and celebrate you every single day. I love you in a way that makes me want to write letters like this — long, emotional, dramatic letters — because you deserve to know how deeply you’re loved. You are so beautiful. And I don’t just mean “pretty.” I mean beautiful in a way that feels rare. Beautiful in a way that makes people stop and notice. Beautiful in a way that shines from the inside out. Your smile, your laugh, your eyes behind your cute glasses, the way you talk, the way you care — it’s all beautiful. You’re beautiful in the way you love people, in the way you show up, in the way you give so much of yourself without even realising it. I appreciate you more than I can ever explain. You support me in ways I don’t think you even realise. You make me feel safe. You make me feel valued. You make me feel like I matter. And I want you to know that I’m always here for you too — always. I’ve got your back through everything. Through the good days, the bad days, the confusing days, the exciting days. I’m here. I’m not going anywhere. You mean so much to me, Kiye. More than you know. More than I probably say out loud. You’re the kind of friend people dream of having. You’re the kind of friend people write poems about, the kind people thank the universe for. You’re the kind of friend who changes lives just by being yourself. And today is your birthday — a whole day dedicated to celebrating you. I hope you feel loved. I hope you feel appreciated. I hope you feel special. I hope you feel the way you make everyone else feel. I hope you realise how important you are, how loved you are, how irreplaceable you are. You deserve the softest days. You deserve the happiest moments. You deserve the biggest dreams. You deserve people who treat you with the same love and loyalty you give so naturally. You deserve to be reminded every single day that you’re enough. More than enough. More than you realise. I adore you, Kiye. I adore your heart, your mind, your soul, your energy. I adore the way you care, the way you love, the way you show up. I adore the way you make everything feel brighter. I adore the way you exist. Thank you for being you. Thank you for being in my life. Thank you for every laugh, every conversation, every moment. Thank you for letting me love you the way I do. Thank you for being my best friend, my person, my everything. Happy birthday, my beautiful girl. Here’s to another year of memories, growth, laughter, and love. Here’s to you — the most precious person I know. I don’t think you realise how much space you take up in my life — not in an overwhelming way, but in the way a favourite song fills a room without even trying. You’ve become one of the safest, softest parts of my world.

Thank you for being my person. Thank you for being you.

With all my love,
Naomi
